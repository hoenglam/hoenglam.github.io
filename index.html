About the project

An exploratory study of how creators and complementary software projects contribute to innovation in digital ecosystems.

The project focuses on innovation ecosystems and digital platforms: how creators and complementary projects interact, how their relationships evolve, and how these connections relate to ecosystem development and sustainability.

The current phase assesses publicly available metadata in the Minecraft modding ecosystem and refines research questions about dependencies, compatibility, and project maintenance. This is an independent, non-commercial academic research project.

An empirical research project examining how mods, creators, and modpacks connect—and how those relationships evolve over time.

FOCUS

DATA

STAGE
Data feasibility assessment and research design
Study scope & data approach+

The initial feasibility collection includes 100 projects selected from two modpack manifests. It is a pilot sample, not a representative sample of the full platform. Historical file observations are drawn from January 2024 archives, with incomplete coverage for some projects.

The next step is to seek authorized research access for a broader and more consistent dataset. Data retention and any sharing of research data will follow the permissions agreed with the data provider. No mod binaries are distributed through this website.

Pilot collection: 11 October 2026 · Research in progress; no findings are reported here.
